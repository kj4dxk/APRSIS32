Sorry, but I just noticed your post. Are you still having the issue, or have you resolved it?

The only reason I know that maps would not show up is if the OSM map tile servers were down (unlikely) or you don't have an active Internet connection. The only setting in APRSISCE/32 that can disable map fetching is if you uncheck Enables / Internet Enabled.
