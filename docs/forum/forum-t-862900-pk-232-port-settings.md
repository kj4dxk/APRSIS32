Hopefully you used Simply(KISS) and not KISS. The former has no initialization/termination commands and assumes that the TNC is already in KISS mode. The latter is provides initialization/termination commands for Kenwood's internal TNCs.

Also, you'll get more answers via the APRSISCE Yahoo group than you will here on the Wiki.
