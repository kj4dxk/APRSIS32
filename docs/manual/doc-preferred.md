# Preferred

The bottom center of the screen communicates what is setting the screen center as follows:

Panning - You've dragged the screen to an arbitrary center

Preferred - The screen zoom and center are at the preferred setting (Screen / Preferred / Save)

Await(call-SS) - APRSISCE/32 is waiting for call-SS to beacon a position which will then be Followed

Track(call-SS) - APRSISCE/32 is tracking the indicated callsign (See also Screen / Follow / Locked)

S - South - APRSISCE/32 is tracking and locked onto Me (Screen / Follow / Me)
