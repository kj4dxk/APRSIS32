**APRS Tier 2 server pools**  
APRSISCE/32 defaults to the rotate.aprs2.net pool on the filter port of 14580

Regional pools are available as follows (from [APRS Tier 2 Network](http://www.aprs2.net/)):  
noam.aprs2.net for North America  
soam.aprs2.net for South America  
euro.aprs2.net for Europe & Africa  
asia.aprs2.net for Asia  
aunz.aprs2.net for Oceania

A full list of Tier 2 servers and their status is available at [T2 Servers](http://www.aprs2.net/serverstats.php)
